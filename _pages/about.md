## About {#about}

I am a postdoc at the University of Washington, advised by [Prof. Tim Althoff](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=yc4nBNgAAAAJ){:.name}. Before that, I completed my PhD at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by [Prof. Xueqi Cheng](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=hY8aLqAAAAAJ){:.name} and [Assoc. Prof. Bingbing Xu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=PSx2drgAAAAJ){:.name}.

My research aims to build trustworthy AI that acts reliably and ethically for social good and human well-being. My current work centers on the alignment and reasoning of large-model agents for long-horizon, open-ended tasks.
